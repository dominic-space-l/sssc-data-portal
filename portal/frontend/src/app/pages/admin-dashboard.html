Send a link without a request

Use these when an organization contacts you directly (e.g. by email) instead of filling in Request access. Then copy the link or email it to them.

New data link

For an organization sending data for the first time, or any organization adding a new station. They fill in one form (contact, organization, project, station, sensor and device), and nothing is saved until you approve it. The link works for one submission and then expires.

So you can tell who their submission is from.
Filled in for you when you click Email it.

Update link

For an organization already in the database that wants to change its stations, contacts, devices or sensors. They can send several changes until the link expires, and you approve each one.

Not in the list? Send them a new data link instead.
